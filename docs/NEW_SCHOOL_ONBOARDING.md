# Starting a new school on SchoolGiant

How a new school (or college) goes from sign-up to its first working day on the system, step by step.
The order matters: each step uses what the step before it set up. For example, students need classes, timetables
need teachers and subjects, and fee bills need fee structures.

Every menu path below is the name the school sees in the app (for example **Administration › Campuses**).

---

## The flow at a glance

| Stage | What gets done | Who does it | Usual time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0. Open the account | Workspace created, first admin login | School owner or our team | 10 minutes |
| 1. School details | Profile, logo, campuses, boards, stamps and signatures | School admin | Day 1 |
| 2. Academic year and classes | Year and terms, classes, subjects, grading | School admin or principal | Day 1–2 |
| 3. Staff and logins | Departments, roles, staff records, logins | School admin, HR | Day 2–3 |
| 4. Students and parents | Student records, parents, ID cards | Office staff | Day 3–5 |
| 5. Teaching set-up | Teacher allocation, timetable, syllabus | Principal, coordinators | Day 4–7 |
| 6. Fees | Fee structures, concessions, online payment | Accounts | Day 4–7 |
| 7. Parent app and messages | App settings, SMS / WhatsApp | School admin | Day 6–7 |
| 8. Go live | First day of attendance, fees and homework | Everyone | Day 7–10 |
| 9. Optional modules | Transport, library, hostel, canteen, payroll… | Each department | When ready |

## Stage 0 — Open the account

There are two ways to open an account:

- **The school signs up itself.** Go to the sign-up page, then enter the school name, the admin's name, email and
  mobile number, and a password. Pick a plan. When email is switched on, a code is sent to that email and the
  account is created only after the code is entered.
- **Our team opens it for them.** Go to **Superadmin › Institutions** and add the school. Use this for a school
  that already has a login with us or is opening a second institution.

The system creates these automatically:

- the school's workspace on a **free trial** (14 days unless the plan says otherwise)
- one campus called **Main campus**
- one role, **Institution Admin**, with full access (this role can't be reduced or deleted)
- the first admin login, holding that role
- starter data the school can change later:
  - the current academic year with two terms
  - a 9-point grading scheme
  - the usual fee heads (tuition, admission, exam…)
  - the usual exams (unit test, half-yearly, annual)

After signing in, the admin lands on **Administration › Setup hub**. Its checklist shows how far set-up has got.

## Stage 1 — School details

| Step | Where | What to fill |
|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | **Administration › Institution profile** | Full legal name, address, phone, email, registration numbers. These print on every receipt, report card and certificate. |
| 1.2 | **Administration › Institution profile** | Logo and brand colour. |
| 1.3 | **Administration › Campuses** | Rename **Main campus** to the real campus name. Add a campus for each extra branch or board. |
| 1.4 | **Administration › Campuses › own school on paper** | Only when a campus prints as a different school: the name printed on paper, board, affiliation number, school code, UDISE code, principal's name, and that campus's logo, seal and signatures. |
| 1.5 | **Administration › Print templates** | The school seal (stamp) and signatures (principal, accountant, class teacher…). Check one receipt and one report card in the preview. |
| 1.6 | **Administration › Number series** | How receipt, admission and certificate numbers look. With more than one campus, each campus gets its own prefix so numbers never repeat. |

### A school that runs two boards (for example CBSE and UP Board)

- Add **one campus per board**, for example "St. Thomas School" (CBSE) and "St. Thomas School – UP Board".
- Each campus gets its **own classes**, so a CBSE class and a UP Board class are never mixed.
- Every campus prints the **same seal and signatures by default**. Fill in step 1.4 only for the campus that should
  print differently. Until then, it uses the school's main ones.

## Stage 2 — Academic year and classes

| Step | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | **Academic setup › Academic years & terms** | Check the year and term dates that were created for the school. Change them to match the school's calendar. |
| 2.2 | **Academic setup › Classes & sections** | Add classes (Nursery, LKG, UKG, Class 1 … Class 12) for each campus. Add sections (A, B…) only where the school has them; a class can run without sections. A long list can be **imported** from a spreadsheet. |
| 2.3 | **Academic setup › Subjects** | Add the subjects for each class (can be imported). |
| 2.4 | **Academic setup › Grading schemes** | Keep the starter scheme or change it to the board's scheme. |
| 2.5 | **Exams & report cards › Exam setup** | Check the exam list (unit tests, half-yearly, annual) and what each one counts for. |
| 2.6 | **Staff & HR › Holidays** | The school's holiday list for the year. Attendance and the timetable skip these days. |

---

## Stage 3 — Staff and logins

| Step | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | **Administration › Departments** | Mathematics, Science, Office, Accounts, Transport… (can be imported). |
| 3.2 | **Administration › Roles & permissions** | Start from the recommended roles (Principal, Teacher, Accountant, Front desk, Librarian…). Change what each role can see and do if needed. |
| 3.3 | **Staff & HR › Staff** | Add every staff member: personal details, photo, joining date, department, designation (can be imported). |
| 3.4 | **Administration › Users** | Give each staff member a login and a role (can be imported). Each person gets a **temporary password** shown once; they must change it at their first sign-in. Hand these out on paper or by message. |

> Changed someone's role later? They need to sign out and sign in again to see the change.

---

## Stage 4 — Students and parents

| Step | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | **Students › Student directory › Import** | For an existing school: import every current student from a spreadsheet (name, class, section, date of birth, parents' names, mobile numbers…). Check the preview, fix any rows marked in red, then import. |
| 4.2 | **Students › New admission** | For new students one at a time, during the year. |
| 4.3 | **Students › Parents & guardians** | Check that every student has at least one parent with a **correct mobile number**. Parents sign in to the app with this number. |
| 4.4 | **Admissions › Admission settings** | Only if the school takes admission enquiries and applications online. |
| 4.5 | **Students › ID cards** | Print ID cards. Each card has the admission-number barcode and a QR code to check the card is genuine. |

---

## Stage 5 — Teaching set-up

| Step | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 5.1 | **Academic setup › Teacher allocation** | Who teaches which subject in which class, and who is the class teacher. Teachers' **My classes** and attendance marking depend on this step. |
| 5.2 | **Timetable › Rooms** | Classrooms, labs, halls. |
| 5.3 | **Timetable › Timetable** | The weekly period-by-period timetable for every class. |
| 5.4 | **Homework & lessons › Syllabus tracker** | Units and topics for each subject, with the planned weeks. Coordinators can do this over the first weeks; it doesn't need to be ready on day one. |
| 5.5 | **Homework & lessons › Subject hub** | Teachers add lesson content for students topic by topic and publish it when ready. They can add this gradually. |

---

## Stage 6 — Fees

| Step | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 6.1 | **Fees › Fee structures** | Fee amounts for each class: tuition, transport, exam, etc., and the due dates (monthly, quarterly or yearly). |
| 6.2 | **Fees › Concessions & scholarships** | Sibling discount, staff ward, scholarships. |
| 6.3 | **Fees › Fee invoices & receipts** | Check this year's fee bills for a few students in each class before the counter opens. **Old dues from before SchoolGiant:** there is no screen yet to enter them as an opening balance. Note them down; this still needs to be built. |
| 6.4 | **Integrations › Payment gateway** | Connect the school's Razorpay account so parents can pay in the app. |
| 6.5 | **Fees › Online payments** | Check that online payments are switched on for parents. |

---

## Stage 7 — Parent app and messages

| Step | Where | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 7.1 | **Parent & student app › App settings** | Switch the app on. Choose what parents see (attendance, homework, fees, results, lessons). Decide whether students get their own login. |
| 7.2 | **Integrations › SMS & WhatsApp** | Connect the SMS / WhatsApp provider so absence alerts, fee reminders and notices reach parents. |
| 7.3 | **Administration › Notification rules** | Which events send a message, and to whom (for example, "student absent" → parent by SMS). |
| 7.4 | **Communication hub › Notices & circulars** | Send a welcome notice that tells parents how to download the app and sign in with their mobile number. |

## Stage 8 — Go live

Do a quick check the day before:

- [ ] The **Setup hub** checklist is complete.
- [ ] Every class has students, a class teacher and a timetable.
- [ ] Every teacher can sign in and sees their classes under **My classes**.
- [ ] One test fee receipt is printed and looks right (logo, address, seal, signature, receipt number).
- [ ] One parent has signed in to the app with their mobile number.

First day:

1. **Teachers** mark attendance (**Attendance › Mark attendance**) in the first period. Parents of absent students get
   an alert.
2. **Office** collects fees at the counter (**Fees › Collect fees**) and closes the day (**Fees › Day-end closing**).
3. **Teachers** open their **teaching day** to see today's periods and topics, mark them Taught / Partly / Not taught,
   and set homework.
4. **Principal** watches the **Dashboard**, which shows what needs action today.

First week:

- Fix anything people get stuck on: missing permissions, wrong mobile numbers, wrong fee amounts.
- When the trial is ending, go to **Administration › Subscription & billing** and choose a plan.

## Stage 9 — Optional modules (switch on when the school is ready)

Every module needs Stages 1–4 done first. Switch modules on or off under **Administration › Modules** (within the plan).

| Module | Set up first |
|---|---|
| Transport | Routes & stops → vehicles & drivers → assign students |
| Library | Catalogue (can be imported) → issue and return |
| Hostel | Rooms → allotment → mess |
| Canteen & wallet | Menu → wallet top-up → orders and tokens |
| Payroll | Salary structures → statutory rules (PF, ESI, TDS) → first payroll run |
| Staff attendance | Check-in areas on the map, or a biometric device under **Integrations › Devices** |
| Exams | Exam schedule → hall tickets → seating → marks entry → report cards |
| Store & assets | Items (can be imported) → opening stock |
| Website | Pages for the school's public website |
| Accounts | Chart of accounts → bank accounts → expenses |

## What the school should have ready before starting

Ask the school to collect these before the first set-up call. Having them ready is what makes it take days, not weeks.

1. Logo (PNG), seal / stamp and signatures (scanned on white paper)
2. Registration details: affiliation number, school code, UDISE code
3. List of classes and sections, and the subjects in each class
4. Staff list: name, mobile number, email, department, designation, joining date
5. Student list: name, class, section, date of birth, admission number, parents' names and mobile numbers
6. Fee chart for each class, plus the concessions they give
7. This year's holiday list and term dates
8. The current timetable
9. Razorpay account details (for online fees) and the SMS / WhatsApp provider (if they have one)

Spreadsheet templates for the imports (students, staff, users, classes, subjects, departments) can be downloaded from
the **Import** button on each of those screens.
